# Pre-P11 Benchmark Archive

This directory contains the benchmark scripts, notebooks, reports, and solver
experiments that existed before the P11A benchmark reset.

They are retained for manual reference only. Do not treat their raw results as
current performance evidence without re-running and recording fresh metadata.

Archived top-level surfaces:

- `runtime/`
- `hotpaths/`
- `nrv_performance/`
- `realistic_examples/`
- `solvers/`
- historical solver experiments such as Triton, CUDA FFI, pseudo-double, and
  archived spikes
- old `src/axonscope/benchmarking` workload/instrumentation files archived as
  `src_benchmarking_benchmark.py`, `src_benchmarking_hotpaths.py`, and
  `src_benchmarking_profiling.py`
- old notebooks and retained reports
